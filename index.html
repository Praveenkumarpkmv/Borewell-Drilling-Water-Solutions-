How We Drill

Our Borewell Drilling Process

A clear, proven four-step process that takes you from dry land to a flowing borewell.

1

Site Survey

We assess your land, study water tables, and pinpoint the best drilling spot.

2

Rig Setup

Heavy-duty rigs are positioned and prepped for safe, efficient drilling.

3

Deep Drilling

4
