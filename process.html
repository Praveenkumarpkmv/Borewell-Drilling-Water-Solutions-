Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does the entire process take?

Typical residential borewells are completed in 24–72 hours from the start of drilling. Projects with additional purification or pipeline work may take up to 5 working days.

What if no water is found at the drilling point?

If our initial survey recommends a point and no water is found within the agreed depth, we provide a second drilling attempt at a nearby point at a reduced rate. Terms are clearly documented in the quote.

Do you service my area?

We cover a wide region. Please check our Service Areas page or contact us — we'll confirm coverage for your location within minutes.

What warranty do you provide?

Drilling work carries a structural warranty. Pumps and purification systems carry manufacturer warranties (typically 1–5 years) plus our own service guarantee on installation quality.
