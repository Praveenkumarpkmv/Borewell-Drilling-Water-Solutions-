Our Story

From One Rig to a Regional Powerhouse

AQUADRILL started in a small garage with a single drilling rig and a promise: to bring reliable, safe water to families who had been let down by unreliable contractors.

Fifteen years later, we've drilled over 2,500 borewells across the region, expanded into full water solutions — pumps, purification, pipelines — and built a team of certified engineers and drilling specialists who share that same promise. Every project we take on carries the same standard: it either works reliably for decades, or we fix it.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Four principles guide every decision we make — from how we quote a job to how we treat the land we drill.

Integrity First

If we can't drill it right, we won't drill it at all. No shortcuts, no upselling, no hidden charges — ever.

Engineering Rigor

Every site is surveyed, every depth is calculated, every system is tested. We trust data over guesswork.

Long-Term Partnership

We don't disappear after the job. Every installation comes with support, warranty, and a team you can call.

Environmental Care

We follow groundwater regulations, protect aquifers, and dispose of drill waste responsibly.
