Common Questions

Before You Reach Out

Answers to the questions we hear most often before starting a project.

Is the site visit really free?

Yes — 100% free. Our engineer visits your location, analyses the site, and gives you an honest recommendation. There's no obligation to proceed.

How quickly will I hear back?

We call back within 24 hours on business days, and usually within a couple of hours for urgent enquiries submitted through the form.

What information should I have ready?

Ideally: your location (city + area), approximate number of people using water, current water source, and any previous borewell information if available. A couple of site photos help too.

Do you handle emergency repairs?

Yes — we have a 24/7 emergency line for pump failures, water pressure drops, and other urgent issues. Call directly for the fastest response.

Prefer to Talk Right Now?

Skip the forms. Give us a ring and speak directly to a certified engineer. No call centers, no wait times — just real answers.
